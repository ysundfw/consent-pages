Privacy policy

jcps-os. Last updated 12 September 2026.

Who this covers

jcps-os is a personal application with a single user: its owner. It is not offered to, and cannot be used by, anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, jcps-os accesses files in the owner's own Google Drive in order to read documents the owner stores there and to write documents the owner asks it to produce.

Where data goes

Nowhere. File content is processed on hardware the owner controls and is stored in the owner's own Google Drive and the owner's own private database. jcps-os does not transmit Drive content to any third party, does not use it to train anything, does not sell it, does not share it, and operates no server that other people can reach.

Credentials

Google OAuth credentials are held encrypted at rest on the owner's own hardware. They are used solely to access the owner's own Drive.

Retention and revocation

jcps-os keeps no copy of Drive content beyond the owner's own storage. Access can be revoked at any time by the owner at Google Account permissions, which immediately and permanently ends this application's access.

Google API Services User Data Policy

jcps-os's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

The owner, at the support address listed on the Google consent screen.
