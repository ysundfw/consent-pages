Privacy policy

Scope

This application has a single user: its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, it accesses files in the owner's own Google Drive, in order to read and write documents there on the owner's behalf.

Use, storage and sharing

File content is used only to perform the actions the owner requests. It is not transferred to any third party, not used to train any model, not sold, and not shared. No copy is retained outside the owner's own storage.

Revocation

The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account, under Data & privacy, in the list of third-party apps with account access. Revoking permanently ends this application's access.

Limited Use

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

The support address shown on the Google consent screen.
